Albany NY Relocation Guide 2026

Everything you need to know before relocating to Albany, Latham, or anywhere in the Capital Region of New York — from a realtor who lives and works here.

Why Move to the Capital Region?

Albany and the Capital Region offer something rare in the Northeast: a high quality of life at a fraction of New York City prices. The area has a thriving economy, four seasons, excellent universities, and some of the most scenic landscapes in the state — the Catskills, Adirondacks, and Hudson Valley all within an hour's drive.

Neighborhoods: Where to Live

Latham — Most Popular for Newcomers

Latham is a hamlet in the Town of Colonie — consistently the most popular destination for relocating families. Excellent schools (South and North Colonie districts), great shopping, dining, and easy access to I-87 and I-90. Median home price ~$360K.

Guilderland — Best School District

If top-ranked schools are your priority, Guilderland is the answer. Quiet, suburban, family-friendly. Median home price ~$382K. Slightly higher price point but excellent long-term value.

Albany City — Urban, Affordable, Walkable

Perfect for state workers, young professionals, and those who want walkable neighborhoods. Neighborhoods like Pine Hills, Center Square, and the Heights offer historic architecture and community. Median home price ~$265K.

Troy — Up-and-Coming

Troy is experiencing a renaissance, with revitalized downtown, restaurants, and arts scene. Great for buyers priced out of Latham or Guilderland. Median home price ~$225K — strong appreciation.

Schenectady — Most Affordable, Growing Fast

Schenectady offers the lowest entry point in the region (~$210K median) with the highest year-over-year appreciation (+11.5%). Good for investors and budget-conscious buyers.
