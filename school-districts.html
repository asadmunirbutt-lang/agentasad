Best School Districts in Capital Region NY

School district quality is the #1 factor for many home buyers in Albany County. Here's everything you need to know about top-rated districts — and what homes cost in each area.

Top-Rated Districts at a Glance

Guilderland Central School District

Consistently ranked #1 or #2 in Albany County. Guilderland High School graduation rate: 97%. Strong AP programs, athletics, and arts. Serves the Town of Guilderland including Altamont.

~$382K
Median home price

Bethlehem Central School District

Top-ranked district in Albany County. Serves Delmar, Slingerlands, and the Town of Bethlehem. Excellent academics and extracurriculars. High college acceptance rates.

~$420K
Median home price

Niskayuna Central School District

Serving Niskayuna in Schenectady County. One of the highest-scoring districts in the region. Strong STEM programs, low student-to-teacher ratio, excellent test scores.

~$355K
Median home price

South Colonie Central School District

Serves Latham, Menands, and surrounding areas. Well-regarded district with growing programs. Great location near shopping, dining, and I-87. Popular with commuters.

~$360K
Median home price

Shenendehowa Central School District

One of the largest and highest-rated districts in Saratoga County, serving Clifton Park and Halfmoon. Excellent sports programs, growing community, and great schools.

~$395K
Median home price

North Colonie Central School District

Serves Loudonville, Latham, and northern Colonie. Highly regarded schools with strong parent involvement. Lisha Kill Middle School and Shaker High School are top-performing.

~$375K
Median home price

How School Districts Affect Home Values

In the Capital Region, homes zoned for top-rated districts command a 10–25% price premium over comparable homes in adjacent districts. Guilderland and Bethlehem homes, for example, are priced significantly higher than similar-sized homes in less sought-after districts — and they hold their value better during market downturns.

If you're buying primarily for school quality, budget accordingly — and let Asad know your priority districts. He can narrow your search to homes in those zones and alert you as soon as new listings hit the market.

How to Verify School Zone Before Buying

  • Ask your realtor to confirm the school district assignment for any specific address
  • Contact the school district directly — district boundaries sometimes differ from zip codes
  • Review GreatSchools.org and NYSED School Report Cards for performance data
  • Visit the school if possible — test scores aren't everything
