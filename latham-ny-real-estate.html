Why People Choose Latham, NY

Latham sits in the heart of the Capital Region — a hamlet within the Town of Colonie that consistently ranks among the most desirable places to live in upstate New York. It's close to everything: Albany International Airport (5 minutes), Downtown Albany (15 minutes), Saratoga Springs (30 minutes), and the Adirondacks (1 hour).

The North Colonie Central School District consistently rates among Albany County's best. Shaker High School is one of the most sought-after public schools in the region — and it directly affects Latham home prices.

Latham Neighborhoods

Blue Barns Road area

Quiet residential streets, larger lots, mix of colonial and split-level homes from the 1970s–90s. Walk to Latham Ridge Elementary. Very family-oriented.

Latham Farms / Route 9 corridor

Newer townhomes and condos, walkable to shopping and dining. Popular with first-time buyers and empty nesters. Higher density, lower maintenance.

Troy-Schenectady Road (Route 2)

Established neighborhood with well-kept ranches and colonials. Strong school assignment (Blue Creek Elementary). Good starter home area.

Airport Road / Watervliet Shaker

Convenient for commuters, close to the airport. Mix of older and newer builds. More affordable entry points with solid appreciation history.

Asad's Local Knowledge:

The most common mistake buyers make in Latham is assuming all of it falls in the North Colonie district. Parts of eastern Latham actually fall in the South Colonie or Lansingburgh districts — which significantly affects home value and your children's school assignment. Always verify the exact school zone before making an offer. Asad double-checks this for every client.
