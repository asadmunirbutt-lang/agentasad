How Asad Sells Your Home

1

Comprehensive Market Analysis

Asad runs a detailed Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) — reviewing recent sold homes, active competition, and local demand trends. Pricing correctly from day one is the most critical factor in getting the best price. Overpriced homes sit; correctly-priced homes spark bidding wars.

2

Pre-Listing Preparation

Small investments before listing yield large returns. Asad advises on which repairs and upgrades pay back the most — a fresh coat of paint, decluttering, landscaping touch-ups, and deep cleaning. Then professional photography is arranged to make your home shine online.

High-ROI Pre-Sale Improvements:
  • Fresh paint (neutral colors): avg. $1,800 → +$5,000–$10,000 in value
  • Curb appeal landscaping: $500 → first impressions drive 7% higher offers
  • Professional staging consultation: $300 → homes sell 73% faster
3

Multi-Channel Marketing

Your home is listed on the MLS (CRMLS/NYSMLS), syndicated to Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, and over 100 real estate websites. Social media ads target active buyers in your price range and preferred school district. Open houses and agent networking complete the strategy.

Expert Offer Negotiation

When offers come in, Asad analyzes each one beyond just the price — contingencies, financing strength, closing timeline, and seller concessions all affect your net. His eCornell Mastery in Negotiations certification puts you in the strongest possible position when multiple offers arrive.

5

Smooth Closing Process

Once under contract, Asad coordinates with the buyer's agent, attorney, lender, and title company to ensure a smooth closing. He keeps you informed at every milestone and handles issues before they become deal-breakers.
