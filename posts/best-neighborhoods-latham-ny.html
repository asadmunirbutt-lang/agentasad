Latham NY Neighborhoods Buying a Home

Best Neighborhoods in Latham, NY (2026 Guide)

By Asad Butt, Realtor  |  September 2026  |  8 min read

Back to Blog

Latham, NY — a hamlet within the Town of Colonie in Albany County — is one of the most sought-after communities in the Capital Region. With excellent schools, easy highway access, proximity to Albany International Airport, and a diverse range of housing options, Latham consistently attracts families, professionals, and first-time buyers. As a local realtor based in Latham, I help buyers find the right pocket of this community almost every week.

Here's my on-the-ground guide to Latham's best neighborhoods in 2026, with real price data, school information, and the lifestyle you can expect in each area.

Why Latham, NY?

Before diving into specific neighborhoods, it's worth understanding why Latham continues to outperform most of the Capital Region in demand and price appreciation.

  • Location: Latham sits at the crossroads of I-87 (Northway) and Route 7 — you can reach downtown Albany in 12 minutes, Saratoga Springs in 30 minutes, and Clifton Park in 15 minutes.
  • Albany Airport: The airport is literally within the hamlet's boundaries — a 5-minute drive for residents, which is a massive advantage for frequent flyers.
  • North Colonie Schools: Most of Latham falls within the North Colonie Central School District, rated among the top 10 districts in the Capital Region. South Colonie district also serves parts of Latham with strong ratings.
  • Shopping & Dining: Latham Circle Mall area, Latham Farms strip (Target, TJ Maxx, BJ's Wholesale), and dozens of local restaurants make it one of the most convenient communities in Albany County.
  • Price Range: Homes typically range from $280,000 for starter ranches to $550,000+ for newer colonial construction, with a median around $362,000 in 2026.

Top Latham Neighborhoods

1. Shaker Road / North Albany Shaker Corridor

$340K–$420KTypical price range
North ColonieSchool district
12 minDrive to downtown Albany

The Shaker Road corridor stretching from Latham toward Loudonville is one of the most established areas in the Town of Colonie. You'll find well-maintained colonials and splits from the 1970s–1990s alongside newer infill construction. Streets like Winding Brook Drive and Shaker Ridge feature larger lots (0.25–0.5 acres), mature trees, and quiet cul-de-sacs.

This area is especially popular with state government employees commuting to the Empire State Plaza — it's close to two major arteries without the highway noise. North Colonie elementary schools in this zone have strong parent ratings, and the middle and high schools (Shaker High School) consistently rank in the top tier statewide.

2. Latham Farms / Central Latham

$280K–$360KTypical price range
North ColonieSchool district
5 minDrive to airport

The heart of Latham — centered around the Latham Farms shopping area on Route 9 — offers the most variety in housing stock. You'll find everything from 1950s cape cods at $270K to fully renovated 4-bedroom colonials at $380K. Streets like Maxwell Road, Watervliet Shaker Road, and Old Loudon Road have excellent walkability for the area.

This is my most frequently recommended area for first-time buyers who want the Latham address without stretching their budget. The homes are slightly older, but lot sizes are generous and renovation potential is high. Most properties are within the North Colonie district, though some streets edge into South Colonie.

3. Newtonville / East Latham (Near Loudonville Border)

$380K–$520KTypical price range
South ColonieSchool district
10 minDrive to downtown Albany

The Newtonville hamlet borders Loudonville to the south and Latham to the north — properties here often market as either, depending on the exact address. This micro-neighborhood commands a premium because of its proximity to private schools, Colonie Town Park, and the Loudonville lifestyle without Loudonville prices.

Homes here are typically larger (2,200–3,200 sq ft), often on half-acre or larger lots, with more custom construction and recent builds. Families willing to stretch their budget find exceptional value compared to Loudonville proper, where the same house can cost $80,000–$150,000 more.

4. Airport Road / Boght Corners Area

$260K–$340KTypical price range
North ColonieSchool district
5 minDrive to major employers

The area north of the airport along Route 9 and Boght Road offers Latham's most affordable entry points. The Boght Corners hamlet (technically part of Colonie but with Latham's zip code) features modest 3-bedroom ranches and splits at prices that are increasingly rare in Albany County.

This is an excellent area for investors or first-time buyers who prioritize school district and location over home size. Properties here tend to sell quickly — often in under 10 days — because the price-to-value ratio attracts both owner-occupants and rental investors.

What's Happening in Latham's Market Right Now (2026)

Latham's market remains firmly a seller's market in 2026. Inventory is tight — typically only 30–50 active listings at any time for the entire hamlet — and well-priced homes receive multiple offers within 7–14 days. The average sale-to-list ratio is 101–103%, meaning buyers should expect to pay at or above asking price for desirable properties.

Price appreciation has averaged 6–8% annually over the past three years, driven by the continued migration of remote workers from NYC and the Hudson Valley who find Latham's combination of price, schools, and infrastructure irresistible compared to their origin markets.

Pro Tip from Asad: In Latham, the difference between a winning offer and losing one often comes down to pre-approval strength and response time. I recommend my buyers get fully underwritten pre-approvals — not just pre-qualification letters — before we start seriously touring. In this market, you may need to make an offer the same day you tour a property.

Schools in Latham, NY

Latham sits primarily within two strong districts:

  • North Colonie Central School District: Covers the majority of Latham. Shaker High School consistently ranks in the top 15% of New York State high schools for college readiness and test scores. The district has five elementary schools with strong parental involvement and community programs.
  • South Colonie Central School District: Covers the southern and eastern parts of Latham near the Loudonville border. Colonie Central High School and Sand Creek Middle School are well-regarded, with strong arts and athletics programs.

Always verify the exact school assignment for any specific address — district boundaries don't always follow street lines in Latham. I can help you confirm this before making an offer.

Commute & Transportation from Latham

  • Downtown Albany: 12–18 minutes via I-90 or Route 9 (no tolls)
  • Albany Airport: 5–8 minutes (residents love this for business travel)
  • Saratoga Springs: 30–35 minutes via I-87 North
  • Clifton Park: 15–20 minutes via I-87 North or Route 9
  • Troy/RPI: 20–25 minutes via I-90 East
  • CDTA Bus: Routes 85 and 87 serve Latham with service to Albany
