Latham, NY

Albany County — Home Base

Latham is a vibrant suburban hamlet in Colonie and the heart of Asad's home territory. With excellent schools, convenient shopping on Troy-Schenectady Road, and easy access to I-87 and I-90, Latham attracts families and professionals alike. Median home prices range from $280K–$420K.

  • Top-rated North Colonie and South Colonie school districts
  • Close to Albany International Airport
  • Mix of ranch, colonial, and new-construction homes
  • Strong demand — homes typically sell in under 30 days

Albany, NY

State Capital — Investment Hub

As New York's state capital, Albany offers a rich blend of history, culture, and opportunity. From the Mansion District's Victorian homes to modern condos in Center Square, Albany's diverse neighborhoods suit every buyer profile. Price points from $150K rowhouses to $600K+ renovated brownstones.

  • Strong rental income potential — major employer hub
  • University at Albany, Albany Medical College nearby
  • Historic neighborhoods: Pine Hills, Helderberg, Delaware Ave
  • First-time buyer programs available through Albany County

Colonie, NY

Largest Town in Albany County

Colonie is the most populous town in Albany County and consistently one of the most desirable places to live in the Capital Region. With outstanding schools, low crime, and excellent infrastructure, Colonie is perfect for families. Typical home prices: $290K–$500K.

  • North Colonie and South Colonie Central School Districts
  • Crossgates Mall, Wolf Road corridor — great amenities
  • Active seller's market with fast-moving inventory
  • Strong community parks, recreation, and safety record
See Colonie Listings
🌳

Guilderland, NY

Top-Rated Schools, Quiet Neighborhoods

Guilderland is prized for its consistently top-ranked Guilderland Central School District and its peaceful, wooded neighborhoods. Close to SUNY Albany and major highways, it attracts academic families and move-up buyers. Median home prices: $330K–$550K.

  • Guilderland CSD ranked among top districts in NYS
  • Minutes from University at Albany campus
  • Variety of neighborhoods: Westmere, Guilderland Center, Altamont
  • Lower property tax rate compared to neighboring towns
See Guilderland Listings
🏛️

Troy, NY

Revitalized Arts & Culture Scene

Troy has experienced a remarkable renaissance. Its stunning 19th-century architecture, thriving downtown dining scene, and affordable price points make it a top choice for first-time buyers and investors. Homes can start as low as $150K with strong appreciation potential.

  • RPI (Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute) drives rental demand
  • Gorgeous brownstones and Italianate Victorians
  • Saturday Farmer's Market — vibrant community life
  • Strong cash-flow rental investment opportunities
See Troy Listings
⚡

Schenectady, NY

Affordable — High Upside

Schenectady offers some of the most affordable entry-level real estate in the Capital Region. With ongoing downtown revitalization, the ALCO site development, and Proctor's Theatre anchoring a cultural revival, Schenectady is gaining momentum. Buy in now before prices catch up.

  • Median home prices starting around $185K–$280K
  • Union College nearby — stable rental market
  • Stockade District — national historic landmark neighborhood
  • Growing employer base: GlobalFoundries, GE Research
