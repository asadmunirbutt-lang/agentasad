Why You Need a Realtor for New Construction

Many buyers mistakenly believe they don't need their own agent when buying from a builder. The builder's on-site agent represents the builder — not you. Here's why having Asad on your side matters:

  • Negotiation: Builders negotiate on upgrades, lot premiums, closing cost contributions, and price — especially on completed inventory homes.
  • Contract review: Builder contracts heavily favor the builder. Asad reviews and explains every clause before you sign.
  • Independent home inspection: Even on new construction, Asad recommends a third-party inspector — issues missed during construction are common.
  • No extra cost: Builder buyer's agent commissions are typically paid by the builder, not the buyer.
  • Timeline management: Construction delays happen. Asad monitors progress and protects your interests throughout.
