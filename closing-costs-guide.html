NY Closing Costs Guide 2026

A complete, itemized breakdown of what buyers and sellers actually pay at closing in Albany, Latham, and the Capital Region of New York.

Buyer Closing Costs in New York

Buyers in New York typically pay 2–5% of the purchase price in closing costs. On a $338,000 home, that's approximately $6,750–$16,900. Here's the itemized breakdown:

Tips to Reduce Closing Costs

  • Shop lenders: Lender fees vary significantly. Get Loan Estimates from 3+ lenders and compare the APR and closing costs, not just the rate.
  • Negotiate seller concessions: In some markets, you can ask the seller to cover a portion of your closing costs — especially if you're offering above asking price.
  • Use first-time buyer programs: SONYMA and DPAL can cover thousands in down payment and closing cost assistance.
  • Close at end of month: Pre-paid interest is charged per day from closing to month-end. Closing later in the month means fewer days of pre-paid interest.
  • Ask about lender credits: A higher interest rate in exchange for lender credits can reduce your out-of-pocket at closing — smart if you plan to move within 5 years.
