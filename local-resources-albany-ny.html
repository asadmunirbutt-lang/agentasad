Albany NY Real Estate Local Resources Directory

Buying or selling a home in Albany requires a whole team. Here are the types of professionals you'll need — what they do, what to look for, and how to find them. Asad can personally connect you with local professionals he works with regularly.

Mortgage Lenders & Banks

Getting pre-approved is the first step. The Capital Region has excellent local banks and credit unions that often beat the big national lenders on rate and service — especially for first-time buyers.

Local Bank

Strong local mortgage division, competitive rates for Capital Region properties. Known for personalized service and quick decisions — useful in Albany's fast-moving market.

First-Time Buyer Programs

SONYMA (State of New York Mortgage Agency)

NYS down payment assistance and low-interest mortgage programs for first-time buyers. Can be combined with federal programs for significant savings. Income and purchase price limits apply.

🌐 hcr.ny.gov/sonyma

Home Inspectors

A thorough home inspection is your best protection as a buyer. Albany's housing stock is old — many homes are from the 1900s–1960s — so choosing an inspector with experience in older homes is critical. Look for inspectors who are members of ASHI or InterNACHI.

What to Look For

Choosing an Inspector in Albany

Key: experience with pre-1970 construction, knob-and-tube wiring, cast iron plumbing, older oil furnaces, and wet basements — all common in Albany's housing stock. Ask specifically about these.

Ask Asad for inspector recommendations →
Specialty Inspections

Beyond the General Inspection

Consider additional inspections for older Albany homes: radon testing (elevated radon is common in Albany County), sewer scope (pre-1980 clay pipes), and mold/air quality if the basement is damp.

Typical cost: $400–$700 for full package

Real Estate Attorneys

New York State requires an attorney for real estate transactions — unlike most other states. Your attorney drafts or reviews the purchase contract, handles the title search, prepares closing documents, and represents you at the closing table. Budget $1,000–$1,800 for attorney fees.

What to Ask When Hiring

Choosing a Real Estate Attorney

  • Do you specialize in residential real estate?
  • How many closings do you do per month?
  • Will you (not a paralegal) handle my file?
  • What is your flat fee, and what does it include?
  • Are you available nights/weekends for deadlines?

Title Companies

In New York, your attorney typically coordinates title through a title insurance company. Owner's title insurance protects your ownership rights against future claims — it's a one-time premium paid at closing and strongly recommended.

National with Local Offices

Fidelity National / First American

Both have Albany-area offices and strong NY operations. Your attorney may work with either — they can shop the rate for you as title premiums are fixed by NY statute but service quality varies.

Owner's Title Insurance

Why You Need It

Albany's old housing stock means decades of ownership history. Errors in past deeds, old liens, and heir disputes do surface. Owner's title insurance is a one-time fee (~0.5% of purchase price) and protects you for as long as you own the home.

Moving Companies

Albany has a number of reliable local movers. Book early — especially for spring/summer moves when demand peaks. Always get at least 3 quotes and verify the company is licensed and insured in New York State.

Moving Tips

Albany Moving Checklist

  • Book at least 4–6 weeks before close date
  • Get binding estimates (not just quotes)
  • Verify NY State DOT moving license
  • Ask about staircase and elevator fees
  • Confirm liability/valuation coverage

Government & Utility Resources

Albany County Clerk

Deed records, tax maps, and property history for Albany County. Useful for researching a property before making an offer.

Property Taxes

Albany County Real Property Tax

Look up assessed values, current tax bills, and exemptions (STAR, senior, veterans) for any Albany County property.

🌐 Albany County Real Property Tax Services
Utilities — Electric

National Grid

Electric provider for most of the Capital Region. Contact to transfer service: 1-800-642-4272. Always request the prior 12 months' utility bills as part of your due diligence — they reveal heating costs.

📞 1-800-642-4272
Utilities — Gas

Niagara Mohawk / National Grid Gas

Natural gas service for most of Albany and surrounding communities. In areas without gas, oil or propane are common — verify fuel type before purchase as it significantly affects operating costs.

📞 1-800-642-4272
Tax Exemptions

NY STAR Program

New York's School Tax Relief (STAR) program provides a partial exemption from school taxes for owner-occupied primary residences. Register at Tax.NY.gov after closing.

🌐 tax.ny.gov
Veterans Benefits

NY Veterans Property Tax Exemption

Eligible veterans and surviving spouses may qualify for a significant property tax reduction in New York. File with your local assessor's office after closing.

🌐 NY State Division of Veterans' Services
