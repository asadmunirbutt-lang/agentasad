Ballston Spa Neighborhoods

Ballston Spa Village

Charming Victorian village center — historic downtown, local restaurants, Saratoga County seat. Walkable and community-oriented.

Route 50 / Suburban areas

More modern suburban development outside the village. Colonial subdivisions, good commuter access.

Galway / Northern area

Rural-suburban transition. Larger lots, agricultural character, equestrian properties.

Charlton Road / Southern

Closer to Clifton Park — benefits from proximity to Shenendehowa corridor without the premium pricing.

Ballston Spa is Saratoga County's value play — you get the county's character and location at pricing well below Saratoga Springs or Clifton Park. Many buyers who can't afford Saratoga Springs discover that Ballston Spa offers a comparable quality of life.

Selling Your Ballston Spa Home in 2026
