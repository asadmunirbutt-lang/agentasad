## The carry-on system that worked ("one adult, two hands, one kid")

- **Backpack on my back at all times** — hands free for Azlan:
  - Outer pockets (5-second access): passports/boarding passes in one fixed zip pocket, snack + Nutella mini + wipes, phone power bank, headphones/tablet for de-escalation, **today's doses in a pill organizer**.
  - Main: both laptops in sleeves (fast pull at security), full med supply in clear ziplock **with prescription copies**, one thin change of clothes for Azlan.
- **One 22″ roller** (opened only on the plane / at YOTEL): kettle, Maggi cups, bread, food kit, my change of clothes, toiletries.
- **Azlan carries nothing** he must be responsible for.
- Security prep: liquids in one clear bag in the front pocket; kettle at the TOP of the roller (screeners ask to see it); laptops slide out in two pulls.

## Hard-won rules (for next time)

1. **Laptops NEVER go in checked luggage** — theft/damage/no airline liability; power banks and spare batteries are forbidden in checked bags outright.
2. Meds + documents + laptops live in the personal item (the one bag that can't be taken away at the gate).
3. Weigh every bag at home (~$10 luggage scale); stop checked bags at ~48 lb.
4. Two bag colors on the carousel beats four identical black bags.
5. Book the airside hourly hotel (YOTELAIR) for long overnight IST layovers — no visa, real bed, shower.
6. Email the hotel ahead about child guest count, payment guarantee, and hot-water needs.
7. Buy luggage to arrive at least a week before the flight; inspect marketplace-seller items the day they arrive.
8. Empty duffels packed flat inside suitcases = free return-trip expansion capacity (Pakistan trips always come home heavier).
