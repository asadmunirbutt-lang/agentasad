Market Conditions: Seller's Market

The Capital Region remains a strong seller's market in 2026, with demand significantly outpacing supply. Months of supply sits at approximately 1.4 months — far below the 5–6 months needed for a balanced market. Key dynamics:

  • Multiple offer situations are common on move-in-ready homes under $400K
  • Homes in top school districts (Guilderland, Bethlehem, Niskayuna) go especially fast
  • New construction is absorbing some demand but supply gaps remain
  • Buyers from NYC and New England are driving competition in Latham and Colonie
  • Cash buyers make up ~22% of transactions, up from 17% in 2024

2026 Market Outlook

The Capital Region's relative affordability compared to New York City and other metros continues to attract buyers. Albany County's diverse employment base — state government, healthcare (Albany Med, St. Peter's), higher education (UAlbany, RPI, Siena, Union College), and growing tech — supports steady housing demand.

Forecasts for the remaining months of 2026 point to continued modest price appreciation (5–8% annualized), stabilizing inventory, and a gradual reduction in multiple-offer situations as more homes come to market in fall. Buyers who act in Q4 2026 may find slightly less competition than Q1–Q2 buyers faced.
