Scotia Neighborhoods

Scotia Village

The walkable core — charming downtown with local shops, restaurants, and the Mohawk River just blocks away.

Western Ave / Main Street

Suburban streets with well-kept colonials and ranches. Established neighborhood feel.

Sanders / Bridge Street area

Close to the Mohawk River bridge to Schenectady. Transition zone with mix of housing types.

Northern Scotia

More residential, quieter. Family-oriented streets with newer and established homes.

Asad's Local Knowledge:

Scotia is a village within the Town of Glenville — not all of Glenville is 'Scotia.' The village proper has a walkable character and community identity that buyers specifically seek. Asad knows the village boundaries and which properties carry the full Scotia premium.

Buying a Home in Scotia NY — What to Expect

Scotia buyers get small-town walkability and riverside character at prices significantly below comparable Albany County communities. First-time buyers in particular find Scotia offering better livability per dollar than most of the region.

Selling Your Scotia Home in 2026

Scotia sellers benefit from the village's strong identity and community appeal. Buyers who want a walkable, small-town environment with Mohawk River access specifically seek out Scotia — it's not just a price point, it's a lifestyle sell.
