First-Time Buyer Programs in NY

New York offers some of the best first-time home buyer programs in the country. Here's everything available to buyers in Albany, Latham, and the Capital Region.

SONYMA — State of New York Mortgage Agency

New York's #1 First-Time Buyer Program

SONYMA offers below-market fixed-rate mortgage loans exclusively for first-time home buyers in New York State. Programs include:

Achieving the Dream

Lowest interest rates for low- to moderate-income buyers. Income limits apply — in Albany County, limits are up to ~$120K for a family of 3+.

Low Interest Rate Program

Below-market 30-year fixed rates for moderate-income buyers. More flexible income limits than Achieving the Dream.

Down Payment Assistance Loan (DPAL)

Up to $15,000 in down payment assistance as a 0% second mortgage. Can be combined with SONYMA first mortgages.

Neighborhood Revitalization

Special incentives for buyers purchasing in targeted revitalization areas — includes parts of Albany and Troy.

FHA Loans — Low Down Payment Option

FHA loans are insured by the Federal Housing Administration and are ideal for buyers with lower credit scores or limited savings. Key features:

  • 3.5% down payment with a credit score of 580+
  • 10% down payment with credit scores 500–579
  • Higher debt-to-income (DTI) ratio allowed — up to 57%
  • Can be combined with SONYMA or local down payment assistance
  • 2026 FHA loan limit in Albany County: $498,257

Albany County & Local Programs

Albany County Rural Housing Alliance

Provides homeownership education, counseling, and down payment assistance for qualifying buyers in Albany County, including Latham, Colonie, and Guilderland.

HOME Investment Partnership Program

Federally funded via the City of Albany — provides down payment and closing cost assistance for qualifying first-time buyers purchasing within city limits.

USDA Rural Development Loans

For buyers purchasing in rural or semi-rural areas. Parts of Albany County qualify. Zero down payment required for eligible properties and buyers.

Conventional Loans with 3% Down

Fannie Mae HomeReady and Freddie Mac Home Possible loans allow qualified first-time buyers to put as little as 3% down on a conventional mortgage — without FHA mortgage insurance premiums once you have 20% equity. These are ideal for buyers with good credit (620+) who want lower monthly costs long-term.
