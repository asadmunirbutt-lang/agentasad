Tips to Get the Best Rate

  • Aim for a credit score of 740 or higher before applying
  • Shop at least 3–5 lenders and compare APR, not just interest rate
  • Consider buying down the rate with discount points if you plan to stay 7+ years
  • Keep your debt-to-income (DTI) ratio below 43%
  • Get pre-approved before making offers — sellers prefer pre-approved buyers
