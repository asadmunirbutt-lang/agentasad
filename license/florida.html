Florida Real Estate License Exam Guide 2026

Step-by-step requirements, exam details, state-specific topics, study tips, and approved course providers for aspiring Florida real estate agents.

Always verify current requirements with the Florida Real Estate Commission (FREC). Requirements change.

At a Glance: Florida Requirements

Requirement Details
Pre-License Education63 hours (Florida-approved courses)
Minimum Age18 years old
Exam VendorPearson VUE
Total Exam Questions100 (45 (national concepts) + 55 (Florida law))
Passing Score75% (75/100)
Exam Fee$36.75
License Application Fee$89
License RenewalEvery 2 years
Continuing Education14 hours (first renewal: 45-hr post-license course)
Regulatory BodyFlorida Real Estate Commission (FREC)
Exam Note: Florida's exam is administered by Pearson VUE. The 100-question exam is not split into separate national/state sections but covers both throughout. You must score 75+ to pass.

Step-by-Step: Getting Your Florida License

1
Complete the 63-hour pre-license course

Take a FREC-approved Sales Associate pre-license course covering Florida real estate principles, practices, and law. Available online or in-person.

2
Apply for the exam through DBPR

Submit your license application to the Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR). Pay the $89 application fee. Background check included.

3
Schedule and pass the Pearson VUE exam

Once your application is approved, schedule your exam at a Pearson VUE test center. Score 75/100 to pass. You have 3.5 hours.

4
Complete fingerprinting

Schedule a Live Scan fingerprinting appointment through an approved vendor. Florida requires fingerprints before issuing the license.

5
Activate license with a Florida broker

Your license starts in "inactive" status. Activate it by affiliating with a licensed Florida broker through the DBPR online portal.

Florida Real Estate Exam — FAQ

How long is the Florida real estate course?
The 63-hour course takes most students 2–4 weeks online at a moderate pace. You must pass a school end-of-course exam before sitting for the state exam.
What is the first-time pass rate for the Florida exam?
Roughly 50–55% of first-time takers pass. The Florida-specific content (FREC rules, Chapter 475) trips up many candidates who don't study the state portion carefully.
Can I get a Florida license if I have a license in another state?
Florida has mutual recognition agreements with several states. If you hold an active license in a recognized state, you may only need to pass the Florida law portion of the exam.
What's the first renewal requirement for new FL licensees?
Within your first 2-year renewal cycle, you must complete a 45-hour post-license course instead of the standard 14-hour CE. This is mandatory — missing it results in license expiration.
How much does it cost total to get a Florida real estate license?
Expect roughly $500–$900 total: course ($149–$500), exam ($36.75), application ($89), fingerprinting ($75–$100), and optional prep materials.
