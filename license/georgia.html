Georgia Real Estate License Exam Guide 2026

Step-by-step requirements, exam details, state-specific topics, study tips, and approved course providers for aspiring Georgia real estate agents.

Always verify current requirements with the GA Real Estate Commission (GREC). Requirements change.

At a Glance: Georgia Requirements

Requirement Details
Pre-License Education75 hours (Georgia-approved courses)
Minimum Age18 years old
Exam VendorPSI Exams
Total Exam Questions152 (100 (national) + 52 (Georgia))
Passing Score72% on each section (72/100 national, 38/52 state)
Exam Fee$121

License RenewalEvery 4 years
Continuing Education36 hours
Regulatory BodyGA Real Estate Commission (GREC)
Exam Note: Georgia has a 152-question exam — one of the largest in the country. Both the national (100 Qs) and state (52 Qs) sections must be passed at 72%. Georgia's license renews every 4 years with 36 hours of CE.

Step-by-Step: Getting Your Georgia License

1
Complete 75-hour GREC-approved pre-license course

Take a 75-hour salesperson pre-license course from a GREC-approved school. The course covers GA license law, agency (BRRETA), contracts, finance, and fair housing.

2
Apply to PSI for the exam

Register for the exam through PSI Exams. Pay the $121 fee. Georgia's exam fee is higher than most states. Schedule at a PSI testing center in Georgia.

3
Pass the 152-question exam

Score at least 72% on BOTH the 100-question national section AND the 52-question Georgia section. Sections must be passed independently.

4
Submit license application to GREC

Apply online at grec.state.ga.us. Submit exam score reports, school certificate, and the $170 license fee. Include your background check authorization.

5
Activate under a GA licensed broker

Your salesperson license must be affiliated with a GREC-licensed broker. Complete the broker affiliation through the GREC online portal.

Georgia Real Estate Exam — FAQ

What is BRRETA and why is it important?
BRRETA (Brokerage Relationships in Real Estate Transactions Act) is Georgia's agency law that defines the types of broker-client relationships allowed in GA. It's heavily tested — know the difference between client and customer status, and the duties owed in each.
Is Georgia a good market for new real estate agents?
Yes — Atlanta metro is one of the fastest-growing markets in the Southeast. Georgia's 4-year license renewal gives new agents more time to get established before their first continuing education requirement.
How long does it take to get a GA license?
Most candidates complete the process in 2–4 months. The 75-hour course typically takes 2–3 weeks online, followed by exam prep and testing.
What happens if I fail one section of the GA exam?
You only need to retake the section(s) you failed. If you pass the national but fail the state (or vice versa), you retake only the failed section. Retakes require paying the exam fee again.
Does Georgia have reciprocity with other states?
Georgia has reciprocity with several states. Active licensees from recognized states may be able to waive the GA pre-license education but must still pass the GA state portion of the exam. Check GREC for the current reciprocity list.
