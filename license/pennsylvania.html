Pennsylvania Real Estate License Exam Guide 2026

Step-by-step requirements, exam details, state-specific topics, study tips, and approved course providers for aspiring Pennsylvania real estate agents.

Always verify current requirements with the PA State Real Estate Commission (PREC). Requirements change.

Exam Note: Pennsylvania's PSI exam has 110 questions split into 80 national and 30 Pennsylvania-specific questions. Both sections must be passed at 70% independently. Total testing time is 3 hours.

Step-by-Step: Getting Your Pennsylvania License

1
Complete 75-hour PREC-approved pre-license course

Take a 75-hour real estate fundamentals course from a PREC-approved school. Topics include PA license law, agency, contracts, finance, and ethics.

2
Register for the PSI exam

Create an account at psiexams.com and register for the Pennsylvania real estate salesperson exam. Pay the $49 fee and schedule at a PSI testing center in PA.

3
Pass the 110-question exam

Score at least 56/80 on the national section AND 21/30 on the PA section. Sections are scored separately; you must pass both.

4
Submit license application to PREC

Apply online through the PA Licensing System (PALS). Submit your exam score, school certificate, and the $107 license fee. Include employment history and any criminal disclosures.

5
Affiliate with a PA licensed broker

A licensed PA broker must sign your application indicating they will employ and supervise you. Interview brokerages in your target market before applying.

Pennsylvania Real Estate Exam — FAQ

What is the Consumer Notice in PA?
The Consumer Notice is PA's required agency disclosure form that agents must present to buyers and sellers at first substantive contact. It explains the types of agency relationships available in PA. This is heavily tested on the PA state section.
Does PA have a separate background check?
PA requires disclosure of criminal history on the application. Certain convictions — especially those involving fraud, deceit, or crimes of moral turpitude — may result in denial. PREC reviews case by case.
How long is the PA pre-license course certificate valid?
Pennsylvania pre-license certificates are valid for 5 years from completion, which is longer than most states.
Is there reciprocity between PA and NJ?
Yes — PA and NJ have a reciprocity agreement. PA licensees may apply for a NJ license (and vice versa) without completing the other state's full pre-license education, subject to passing the state portion of the exam.
What is the PA real estate exam pass rate?
Roughly 55–60% of first-time test takers pass. The state section, which focuses on RELRA and the Consumer Notice, is where most candidates struggle. Study PA-specific material separately.
