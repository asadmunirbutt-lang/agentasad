New York Real Estate License Exam Guide 2026

Step-by-step requirements, exam details, state-specific topics, study tips, and approved course providers for aspiring New York real estate agents.

Always verify current requirements with the NY Dept. of State (DOS). Requirements change.

Exam Note: The NY exam uses a combined format — 75 questions covering both national and state law. There is no separate national/state score; you need 53 correct to pass.

Step-by-Step: Getting Your New York License

1
Complete 77-hour pre-license course

Take a DOS-approved 77-hour salesperson course at an accredited school or online provider. Cover real property, law of agency, legal issues, finance, valuation, human rights, and fair housing.

2
Submit exam application to PSI

Register for the exam through PSI Exams (the NY exam vendor). Pay the $65 fee. You can schedule at any PSI testing center across New York State.

3
Pass the 75-question exam

Score at least 53 out of 75 questions. You have 1.5 hours. Both national concepts and NY-specific law are tested in one combined exam.

4
Complete your license application

Apply through the NY DOS eAccessNY portal. Submit proof of education, exam results, and the $50 license fee. You must be 18+ and have a high school diploma or GED.

5
Affiliate with a sponsoring broker

Your NY salesperson license must be activated under a licensed real estate broker. Interview multiple brokerages before signing. The broker's name appears on your license.

New York Real Estate Exam — FAQ

How long does the NY real estate course take?
The 77-hour course can be completed in as little as 2 weeks full-time, or 6–8 weeks part-time. Online courses let you go at your own pace within a set window (usually 6 months).
Can I take the NY real estate exam online?
Currently, the NY state exam must be taken in person at a PSI testing center. The pre-license course can be completed fully online.
How soon can I retake the NY exam if I fail?
You can reschedule through PSI after a failed attempt. There's no mandatory waiting period, but you must pay the $65 fee again. Your course certificate is valid for 8 years from completion.
Do I need a sponsor before I take the exam?
No — you can take the exam before finding a sponsoring broker. However, your license won't be activated until a broker affiliates you in the DOS system.
What is the NY real estate exam pass rate?
Approximately 55–60% of first-time test takers pass. Consistent practice exam performance of 80%+ is the best predictor of success.
